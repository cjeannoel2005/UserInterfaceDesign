LinkedIn is the world's largest online professional networking platform used for career development, job searching, and business connections.

LinkedIn serves as a comprehensive ecosystem for professional growth by blending a dynamic digital resume with powerful networking and recruitment tools. The platform empowers individuals to showcase their career achievements and discover job openings, while simultaneously allowing businesses to cultivate their brand image and hire top talent. Beyond job hunting, it functions as a global knowledge-sharing hub where professionals publish industry insights, expand their networks, and establish industry authority.
